## Conclusion

**Directionally supported, with one weak subperiod.** The BAB factor is *positive* (excess return, FF3 and FF4 alpha all > 0) in all four subperiods, and is strongly significant (|t|>1.96) in 3 of 4. The exception is **Subperiod 1 (1928-08 .. 1948-12)**: positive but not significant at 5% (excess t=0.91, FF3 t=1.71). This is consistent with the paper's claim in direction; the shortfall in the first subperiod's significance is expected because (a) our BAB series starts 1928-08 (beta-estimation warmup, assumptions A6/A8), omitting the first ~2.5 years of the paper's 1926-based first window, and (b) data-vintage differences. Subperiods 2-4 are highly significant (t≈3.4-6.2), matching the paper's 'significant positive return' claim.
